Solve any face-turning octahedron (FTO) scramble right here in your browser. Hold the puzzle with a corner pointing at you: front faces U (top), R, F (bottom), L; back faces B (top), BR, D (bottom), BL. X turns face X clockwise as seen from outside, X' anticlockwise.

  More options and how it works
  
On your first visit the page prepares its solving data (about 134 MB), which
  takes a minute or so, and saves it in this browser, so later visits start almost at once.
  A random scramble gets a solution of about 22–24 moves within seconds. Proving that a solution
  is the shortest possible can take hours for a random scramble, but short scrambles get their
  shortest solution right away. Using more of your computer's processor cores makes it faster.
