omelettehedron

Solve any face-turning octahedron (FTO) scramble right here in your browser, or a scrambled puzzle from its colours. Hold the puzzle with a corner pointing at you: front faces U (top), R, F (bottom), L; back faces B (top), BR, D (bottom), BL. X turns face X clockwise as seen from outside, X' anticlockwise.

Hold your puzzle with a corner pointing at you and colour the front picture as you see it: U at the top, R on the right, F at the bottom, L on the left. Then turn the whole puzzle around, keeping the top at the top, and colour the back picture: B at the top, BL on the right, D at the bottom, BR on the left. Pick a colour, then click or drag over the stickers. The colours under the picture are your solved puzzle as you usually hold it to scramble: at first the standard colours held the standard way, white on U and green on F (red R, purple L, blue B, orange BL, yellow D, grey BR), as on Diansheng, Dayan and most FTOs. For a LanLan FTO (red and green swapped) choose LanLan colours; for any other puzzle or hold, press Change colours and set each face's colour. Solutions are right whatever the colours; they only matter for the scramble from your usual hold.


  

  

  






  More options and how it works
  
The first time, the page prepares its solving data here in your browser and
  saves it, so later visits start almost at once. There are two kinds. Standard
  (134 MB) takes about a minute to prepare. Faster (about 1 GB) takes several
  minutes and needs a computer with plenty of free memory (phones and tablets may not manage
  it), but then solves faster: proving that a solution is the shortest possible takes about a
  fifth of the time. If Faster cannot be prepared, the page goes back to Standard. Each kind is
  saved separately, so you can switch between them.

  
A random scramble gets a solution of about 22–24 moves within seconds. Proving
  that a solution is the shortest possible can take hours for a random scramble, but short
  scrambles get their shortest solution right away. Using more of your computer's processor
  cores makes it faster.
